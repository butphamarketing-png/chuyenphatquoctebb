Shipping to the United States

B&B sends to the United States with DHL, FedEx and UPS, and picks up in Ho Chi Minh City. The website has a sample price per kilogram for this lane. That number is an estimate, not the official rate card.

The route page says about 5–10 days. The actual day depends on the carrier, the flight, and whether US customs holds the parcel.

Open the quote page, choose the United States, and enter the kilograms. To confirm a rate, call 0941 192 193.
