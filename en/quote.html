Questions

Is this the B&B rate card?

No. Sample figures exist for the United States, Canada, Australia, the United Kingdom and Germany. South Korea, Japan and every other country are quoted by phone on 0941 192 193.

What does the VAT box do?

It multiplies the sample by 1.08 so you can see the figure. It is not a tax invoice.

What about express?

Express changes with the carrier’s fuel surcharge. Call to confirm.

Over 500 kg, or sea freight?

The box stops at 500 kg. Heavier lots and sea freight are quoted by phone. There is no sea rate card on the website.
