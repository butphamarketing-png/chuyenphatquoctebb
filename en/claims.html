Claims

Compensation follows the carrier’s terms and the value you declared. This page does not state a fixed amount.

Parcels sent with DHL, FedEx or UPS are considered under that carrier’s conditions and the value declared at pickup. A low declaration limits the claim to the declared figure.

If a parcel is lost or damaged

  • Keep the tracking number, a photo of the parcel at pickup, and a photo on delivery if you still have it.
  • Tell B&B while the carrier still accepts a file. Call 0941 192 193 or use Zalo on the same number.
  • B&B says which papers are needed. The carrier decides the claim.

Usually outside a claim

  • Prohibited goods, a wrong declaration, or batteries that were not mentioned.
  • Delay, when the carrier does not pay for delay.
  • Missing papers the sender cannot supply.

Extra insurance for a high-value shipment is quoted before pickup. The fee is not listed on the website.
